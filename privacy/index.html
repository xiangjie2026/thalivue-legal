Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

We collect nothing.

THALIVUE works completely offline. No accounts. No servers. No tracking. Your dive life belongs to you alone.

01Who we are

THALIVUE (the "app") is a fully offline dive logbook and digital passport, distributed on the Apple App Store under the identifier com.lulle.thalivue. This policy explains how the app handles data — which, by design, is barely at all.

02Data, in summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or process any personal data:

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection
  • No analytics, no behavioral tracking, no advertising, no ad identifiers (IDFA)
  • No third-party SDKs, no data brokers, no cloud servers of ours

Your dive log, photos and settings exist on your device only. There is no server of ours where your data could exist.

03Permissions

  • Photo library (add only) — used when you choose to save a dive share card or PDF passport to your photos. The app never reads your photo library.
  • Photo import — you pick photos through Apple's system photo picker; the app receives only the photos you select.
  • Location (while using) — used only to suggest nearby dive sites when you ask. Location is used on-device in the moment; it is never stored or uploaded.

04About Pro (in-app purchases)

THALIVUE Pro purchases and refunds are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details, or purchase records. Apple's standard End User License Agreement applies. Purchases can be restored on any device with your Apple ID.

05Where your data lives, and your control

All data (dives, sites, photos, preferences) is stored on this device only. Deleting the app permanently deletes all of it. Exports (CSV / UDDF / PDF) are ordinary files you control — wherever you send them is your choice, not ours. If you use iCloud device backup, your backup is governed by Apple's backup mechanism; we have no access to it.

06Children

THALIVUE is rated 4+. We collect no data from anyone, of any age.

07Changes & contact

This policy may be revised with app updates; the current version always lives on this page. Questions: thalivue@zohomail.com
